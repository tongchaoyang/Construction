About Landmark Construction & ADU

里程建筑 — Building trust, one project at a time.

Landmark Construction & ADU is a licensed general building contractor based in San Jose, California, serving homeowners across Silicon Valley. We specialize in new home construction, Accessory Dwelling Units (ADUs), home remodeling, and earthquake retrofit work.

Our bilingual team works closely with English- and Chinese-speaking clients throughout the Bay Area, guiding every project from initial design through permitting, construction, and final walkthrough.
